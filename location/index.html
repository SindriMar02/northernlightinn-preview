Location

Just beforethe Blue Lagoon

20 minutes from Keflavík International Airport and 45 from Reykjavík.

Getting here

Road 41,then road 43

Take road 41, the road between Keflavík airport and Reykjavík, and turn onto road 43, Grindavíkurvegur. Some navigation apps may still show outdated routes, so follow the road signs to the hotel.

Blue Lagoon

A complimentary shuttleevery hour

The hotel runs a complimentary scheduled shuttle for its guests. Book the Blue Lagoon itself in advance, as it tends to sell out.

  • To the lagoonEvery hour on the hour, by request at the front desk, until 21:00
  • Back to the hotel12:05, 14:05, 16:05, 18:05, 19:05, 20:05 and 21:05

Northern lights

Step outsideand look up

Away from city lights, the hotel is a good place to see the northern lights. The aurora season runs from the end of August until the first week of April. Sign up at the front desk for an aurora call to your room.

Airport transfer

A discounted taxito and from the airport

The hotel works with the taxi service A-Stöðin. Discounted rates are reserved 24 hours ahead for arriving guests and the evening before for departing guests, one trip per room each way.

The area

Open, andwell prepared

The hotel is open. It stands in a seismically active area and has detailed safety measures in place, which it describes on its own website.
