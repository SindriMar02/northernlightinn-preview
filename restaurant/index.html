Max's Restaurant

Breakfast, lunchand dinner, every day

Nordic flavours and the best of local ingredients.

The name

Named afterthe family dog

Kristjana Einarsdóttir, co owner of Northern Light Inn, created Max's Restaurant for her guests. It is named in honour of the family's golden retriever, who loved the guests and good food, and watching the aurora.

Chef Rudolf Vaslaki prepares Nordic flavours and local ingredients with international style.
